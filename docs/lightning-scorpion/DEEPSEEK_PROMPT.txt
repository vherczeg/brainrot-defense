- Portrait icons: `public/assets/icons/lightning-scorpion-v1.png`, `lightning-scorpion-v2.png`, `lightning-scorpion-v3.png`

The spritesheets are horizontal RGBA PNGs, each 1024×256, with four 256×256 frames. Idle and walk loop; attack and hurt play once. Separate frame PNGs are also included under `frames/`. Unit origin is `(0.5, 0.875)`, effect origin `(0.5, 0.5)`. Use Vite's `BASE_URL` for public asset paths.
